# Initial source register

Source selection prepared 5 October 2026 from the existing project archive. This is not a new exhaustive search. Printed page numbers can differ from scan viewer indices.

| ID | Source | Use and limits |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | [woodson600's discovery thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/vintagecomputing/comments/1wtwck7/found_this_old_humdinger_computer_at_goodwill/) | Origin of discovery photographs and finder's public account; ownership history before Goodwill unknown. |
| S02 | [Home Computing Weekly no. 4, 29 March 1983](https://archive.org/details/home-computing-weekly-004), cover and p.5 | Names Roger Pitkin as Venture Micro president; announced pricing and expansion proposals. |
| S03 | [Peter Rodwell, PCW June 1983, p.132](https://archive.org/details/PersonalComputerWorld1983-06/page/n135/) | Faire eyewitness coverage; not proof of customer deliveries. |
| S04 | [Tim Hartnell, Stateside, ZX Computing June/July 1983, p.54](https://archive.org/details/zxcomputing-magazine-1983-06/page/n53/) | Orders by Hartnell and Richard Turner; Hartnell's delivery still pending at time described; sound praise. |
| S05 | [David H. Ahl, Creative Computing June 1983](https://www.atarimagazines.com/creative/v9n6/180_The_8th_West_Coast_Comput.php) | 8 KB BASIC ROM specification, base computer witnessed, peripherals on flyer/price list. |
| S06 | Radio-Electronics June 1983 p.138, Quest advert | $129.95, 4K RAM, 8K BASIC ROM, music/sound. Clear scan held privately. Comparable advert reproduced in [House hearings p.276](https://books.google.com/books?id=XkOcTJATCxgC&pg=PA276). Advertising does not prove delivery. |
| S07 | InfoWorld, 11 April 1983, p.8, Computer Faire report | Centronics, RS-232, joystick, cartridge, RF out, external speaker. Scan examined in project; direct public scan link to be added when verified. |
| S08 | [Motorola MC6847/MC6847Y datasheet](https://people.ece.cornell.edu/land/courses/ece4760/ideas/mc6847.pdf), mode table p.18 | Chip capabilities, not verified Humdinger firmware modes. |
| S09 | [Motorola Micro Chroma 68, October 1979](http://bitsavers.informatik.uni-stuttgart.de/components/motorola/6800/MicroChroma_68_Oct79.pdf) | Comparative reference design only; no demonstrated Humdinger ancestry. |

Nick's 2020 research history and the 2026 transfer/project status are his own accounts. The current status is dated; arrival is not recorded as complete.

The initial public account intentionally omits speculative personal identifications and unverified company-history conclusions. The fuller private research pack remains available to the custodian for review.
