# Contributing to Doppledinger

Useful contributions include first-hand recollections, original documentation, technical observations and corrections with sources.

Open an issue for one specific question or lead. Include the source, date, printed page or photograph identifier where possible. Distinguish what you observed from what you infer. Submit document or code changes through a pull request so the evidence and change can be reviewed together.

Use these evidence descriptions consistently:

- **Observed:** readable photograph detail or a recorded measurement. State which.
- **Reported:** a contemporary publication or attributed account.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposed explanation awaiting evidence.
- **Unknown:** not established by this project.

A product advertisement does not establish that a feature shipped. An unsuccessful search does not establish that a document never existed. Do not identify living people by speculative same-name matches or post private contact details.

Share only material you have permission to share. Please do not upload private correspondence, shipping labels or restricted collections. For large photographic collections, agree on an archival location first and contribute a catalogue and source links here.

Keep changes focused. Explain what changed and what evidence supports it. Physical intervention on the original machine is the custodian's decision; a suggestion in an issue is not an instruction to perform it.


## Standing contribution terms

By intentionally submitting your original research writing, documentation or photographs for inclusion in Doppledinger under this policy, you agree to license your contribution under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). You retain copyright and confirm that you have the right to offer the contribution under those terms. Reuse, adaptation and commercial use are permitted subject to the licence, including attribution and identification of changes.

This is the standing policy: no separate agreement, checkbox or repeated confirmation is required for each pull request or submission. Please give your preferred credit name or pseudonym; we record authorship and photo credits alongside the work. Contributions made before this policy was communicated are not automatically relicensed.

Website code contributions use MIT instead. Future hardware and firmware contributions will use their explicitly stated licences; this CC policy does not cover them.

Clearly identify third-party material and its source and licence or permission. Supplying a scan, ROM dump or someone else’s photograph does not grant us rights you do not hold. If different terms are proposed, state them before inclusion so they can be reviewed. Mere correspondence or sharing a research lead is not automatically a submission for publication.

For contributions arriving outside GitHub, link this policy when inviting the contribution rather than requesting a new agreement for each file.
