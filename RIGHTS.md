# Rights and attribution

Licence scopes and third-party credits for the public project.

## Project licensing decisions — 5 October 2026

- **Website code: MIT**, approved by Nick. This covers the original HTML structure and CSS, not the embedded editorial text, photographs or other third-party assets.
- **Original project research writing, documentation and Nick’s original photographs: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**, approved by Nick. Except where separately identified, these original contributions are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. This includes original editorial text embedded in the website, but excludes third-party material.
- **Future original hardware designs: plan to use CERN Open Hardware Licence v2.** The permissive, weakly reciprocal or strongly reciprocal variant remains to be selected when the designs are prepared for release. This statement of intent is not a licence grant over historical Humdinger designs.
- Future firmware licensing will be specified separately; recovered historical ROMs do not become MIT-licensed merely by inclusion.

The repository uses explicit scopes for its different licences. See [LICENCE.md](LICENCE.md).

The two photographs in `docs/assets/` are by Aarin / woodson600, from the original Reddit discovery post. Published with Aarin's permission, confirmed by Nick on 5 October 2026. Copyright remains with the photographer; they are excluded from the project's CC BY and MIT grants. See [photograph credits](photos/README.md).

Historical magazines, manuals, logos and other third-party material retain their own rights. This package links to source publications and does not include the full magazine/research archive.

Shipping labels, tracking numbers, private contact details and correspondence are not part of this package.
